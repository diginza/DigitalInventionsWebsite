HOW WE HELP

Three ways we work with you

Support for a significant moment, a defined project or the work that continues every day.

CONVENE

Impact Events & Convenings

Bring people together for learning events, programme launches, grantee convenings and milestone events, with digital and knowledge-led support from planning to follow-through.

We support registration, communications and participant engagement, then capture the insights and reflections generated along the way. These become post-event synthesis and useful learning products that extend the value of your gathering.

Focused Projects

ENABLED BY ACTION WEAVER

Digital tools built around the work

Where technology can make Mission Assets more useful, we design and deploy fit-for-purpose digital tools using Action Weaver, our configurable platform for data collection, engagement, learning and knowledge capture.

Action Weaver can support programmes, communities of practice, events and organisational workflows. We use it where it fits the needs of your event, assignment or ongoing support.

Bring data collection, participant engagement, communications and knowledge capture into the workflows your team needs. The starting point is your work and the people doing it.

A system that collects and analyses Mission Asset data in real time can support implementation evaluation and reporting while a programme is under way. This helps teams understand what is being delivered, identify challenges and make timely adjustments. It brings attention to an often-neglected part of MERL: learning from implementation as it happens, alongside evaluating and reporting on impact after implementation.
